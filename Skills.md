# Skills Catalog

> Competencies required across the marketing organization, grouped by domain.

---

## Strategy
- **Brand Strategy** — Positioning, differentiation, brand architecture
- **Market Research** — Qualitative/quantitative research, competitive analysis
- **Go-to-Market Planning** — Launch strategy, segmentation, messaging frameworks
- **Business Acumen** — P&L understanding, revenue modeling, budget management
- **Audience Segmentation** — Persona development, TAM/SAM/SOM analysis
- **Competitive Intelligence** — Market mapping, SWOT, battlecard creation

## Creative
- **Visual Design** — Layout, typography, color theory, brand systems
- **Copywriting** — Headlines, long-form, UX copy, tone of voice
- **Art Direction** — Campaign visual concepts, photo/video direction
- **Video Production** — Scripting, shooting, editing, motion graphics
- **Brand Identity** — Logo systems, style guides, brand books
- **Storytelling** — Narrative structure, emotional hooks, brand narratives

## Technical
- **Marketing Automation** — HubSpot, Marketo, Pardot workflow design
- **CRM Management** — Salesforce, HubSpot CRM, pipeline management
- **HTML/CSS** — Email templates, landing page customization
- **API Integrations** — Zapier, webhooks, data connectors
- **Tag Management** — GTM, pixel implementation, event tracking
- **A/B Testing** — Experimentation design, statistical significance

## Analytics
- **Web Analytics** — GA4, Adobe Analytics, attribution modeling
- **Data Visualization** — Looker, Tableau, Power BI, dashboard design
- **SQL & Data Querying** — Database queries, data extraction
- **Statistical Analysis** — Regression, cohort analysis, forecasting
- **Attribution Modeling** — Multi-touch attribution, media mix modeling
- **Reporting** — Executive dashboards, weekly/monthly reporting cadences

## Digital Marketing
- **SEO** — Technical SEO, content optimization, link building
- **SEM/PPC** — Google Ads, Bing Ads, bid strategy, quality score
- **Paid Social** — Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads, TikTok Ads, audience targeting
- **Email Marketing** — Deliverability, segmentation, lifecycle campaigns
- **Content Marketing** — Editorial planning, content distribution, repurposing
- **Social Media** — Platform strategy, community management, social listening

## Communications
- **Public Relations** — Media relations, press releases, media training
- **Crisis Communications** — Rapid response, stakeholder messaging
- **Corporate Communications** — Internal comms, executive comms
- **Influencer Relations** — Partnership negotiation, campaign management
- **Event Management** — Trade shows, webinars, executive events

## Leadership
- **Team Management** — Hiring, coaching, performance reviews
- **Cross-functional Collaboration** — Working with Sales, Product, Engineering
- **Stakeholder Management** — Executive reporting, board presentations
- **Vendor Management** — Agency relationships, contract negotiation
- **Budget Management** — Forecasting, allocation, ROI optimization
- **Change Management** — Process improvement, org transformation
