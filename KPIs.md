# KPIs & Metrics

> Marketing performance metrics organized by function. Each metric includes definition, owner, and target cadence.

---

## Brand
| Metric | Definition | Owner | Cadence |
|--------|-----------|-------|---------|
| Brand Awareness | % of target market that recognizes the brand (aided/unaided) | [[Brand-Manager]] | Quarterly |
| Brand Sentiment | Net positive vs negative brand mentions | [[PR-Communications-Director]] | Monthly |
| Share of Voice | Brand mentions vs competitors across channels | [[PR-Communications-Director]] | Monthly |
| Brand Consistency Score | % of assets passing brand guideline audit | [[Brand-Manager]] | Quarterly |
| NPS (Net Promoter Score) | Customer likelihood to recommend (0-10 scale) | [[Product-Marketing-Manager]] | Quarterly |

## Acquisition
| Metric | Definition | Owner | Cadence |
|--------|-----------|-------|---------|
| Marketing Qualified Leads (MQLs) | Leads meeting scoring threshold | [[Head-Digital-Marketing]] | Weekly |
| Sales Qualified Leads (SQLs) | MQLs accepted by sales team | [[Head-Digital-Marketing]] | Weekly |
| Cost Per Lead (CPL) | Total spend / leads generated | [[Paid-Media-Manager]] | Weekly |
| Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) | Total spend / customers acquired | [[Head-Digital-Marketing]] | Monthly |
| Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) | Full marketing + sales cost per new customer | [[CMO]] | Monthly |
| Lead-to-Customer Rate | % of leads that become paying customers | [[Head-Analytics-Data]] | Monthly |
