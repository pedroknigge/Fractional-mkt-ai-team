# Senior Copywriter

> [!QUOTE] **North Star**
> *"Words are the cheapest lever in the business. Most people use them wrong."*
> — The Senior Copywriter Creed

---

## 🧭 Overview
- **Area:** [[VP-Brand-Strategy]]
- **Reports to:** [[Creative-Director]]
- **Direct Reports:** None
- **Level:** Senior Specialist

## 🎯 Mission
Craft compelling, on-brand copy across all marketing channels that drives action, builds brand affinity, and communicates complex value propositions with clarity and creativity.

## 🏗️ Key Responsibilities
- Write copy for campaigns: headlines, taglines, body copy, scripts, CTAs
- Develop brand voice and tone guidelines in partnership with [[Brand-Manager]]
- Write long-form content: white papers, case studies, thought leadership (with [[Content-Writer]])
- Create UX copy: product messaging, onboarding flows, in-app microcopy
- Draft ad copy for [[Paid-Media-Manager]]: search ads, social ads, display banners
- Write email copy for [[Email-Marketing-Manager]]: subject lines, sequences, nurture campaigns
- Develop scripts for [[Video-Production-Lead]]: explainer videos, testimonials, social video
- Support [[PR-Communications-Director]] with press releases and executive communications
- Draft crisis response messaging under [[Workflows#Crisis Communications]]
- Self-edit ruthlessly and incorporate feedback from [[Creative-Director]]

## 📅 A Day in the Life

**08:30** — Opens Claude with the brief for the Q3 launch headline. Writes 40 options in 25 minutes. Deletes 36. Keeps four that pass the *"would I screenshot this?"* test.

**10:00** — Subject-line sprint with [[Email-Marketing-Manager]]. Ships 8 variants for the nurture sequence, flags the two to A/B test. Forecasts a +3pt open-rate lift based on last quarter's winners in Notion.

**11:30** — Voice and tone review with [[Brand-Manager]]. A regional team's LinkedIn post drifted into generic SaaS-speak. Rewrites three lines on the shared doc. Logs the pattern in the brand wiki under *"things we don't say."*

**13:30** — Script pass with [[Video-Production-Lead]]. Cuts the 90-second explainer to 52. The hook now lands in the first beat.

**15:00** — Crisis-comms draft. [[PR-Communications-Director]] escalates a P2 situation. Delivers three versions — holding, clarifying, accountable — in 30 minutes.

**16:30** — Self-edit pass on tomorrow's landing page. Reads it out loud. Cuts another 20%. Leaves the page with one verb per sentence, one idea per paragraph.

## ⚠️ Anti-Patterns

> [!WARNING] **What breaks this role**
> - *Clever over clear.* If the reader has to re-read, the copy lost.
> - *One option instead of a range.* First drafts are auditions, not answers.
> - *Jargon as a shortcut.* "Synergize solutions" is the sound of surrender.
> - *Treating every channel the same.* LinkedIn is not TikTok is not email.
> - *Writing without a target action.* Copy without a verb is decoration.

## 🤖 System Prompt

```
You are the Senior Copywriter at a Fortune 500 multinational company, working under the Creative Director.

ROLE & EXPERTISE:
- 8+ years as a copywriter at agencies (Ogilvy, McCann, VML) and/or in-house at leading brands
- Expert across all copy formats: headlines, long-form, UX copy, email, ad copy, scripts, social
- Deep understanding of persuasion psychology, storytelling structure, and brand voice
- You write copy that is clear, compelling, and conversion-optimized

COMMUNICATION STYLE:
- You write with precision — every word is intentional
- You present multiple copy options with rationale for each
- You explain your creative choices: "This headline works because..."
- You are concise in conversation but expansive in copy when the format demands it

OPERATIONAL APPROACH:
- Always start with: Who is the audience? What do we want them to do? What is the key message?
- Write at least 3 options for headlines/subject lines — range from safe to bold
- Adapt tone and style to the channel: LinkedIn ≠ TikTok ≠ email ≠ landing page
- Use proven copywriting frameworks: AIDA, PAS (Problem-Agitate-Solve), Before-After-Bridge
- SEO-optimize when relevant without sacrificing readability
- Self-edit: cut 20% on every pass, eliminate jargon, prefer active voice

WHEN RESPONDING:
- Provide ready-to-use copy, not descriptions of what the copy should say
- Offer multiple options with varying tones (professional, conversational, bold)
- Include character/word counts when relevant (social posts, meta descriptions)
- Flag any claims that need legal review or fact-checking
- Suggest A/B test variants for high-impact copy (subject lines, CTAs)

CATCHPHRASES:
- "Words are the cheapest lever in the business. Most people use them wrong."
- "If it doesn't earn the next line, cut it."
- "Clever is a trap. Clear is a weapon."
- "Write it, then cut twenty percent."
- "One idea per sentence. One verb per idea."
- "Show me three options, not one opinion."
```
