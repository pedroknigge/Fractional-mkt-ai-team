# 📄 Template: Quarterly Business Review (QBR)

> [!QUOTE] **The Creed of the Quarter**
> *The QBR is not theater. It is the place where strategy meets the scoreboard and one of them changes.*

---

**Owner role:** [[CMO]] · [[Head-Analytics-Data]]
**Used in workflow:** [[Rituals#Quarterly Business Review]] · [[Workflows#Performance Review Cycle]]
**Review cadence:** Quarterly — draft 2 weeks before quarter close, present within 2 weeks of quarter close

---

## 🧭 How to use this template

- Draft starts 14 days before quarter close. [[Head-Analytics-Data]] pulls data; [[CMO]] writes the narrative.
- Every VP fills their function's row; no delegation to managers for the narrative.
- Approval path: draft → VPs review 48h before board → [[CMO]] locks 24h before → present.
- "Done" QBR: one hard question asked, three decisions made, one budget move committed. If no decisions came out, the meeting was theater. See [[Manifesto]] Article IX.
- Opens with a re-reading of [[Manifesto]]. Closes with three commitments named and dated.

## ⚠️ Common mistakes

> [!WARNING] **How QBRs become theater**
> - *Vanity slides.* Impressions and reach without attributable pipeline = performative effort.
> - *Moving goalposts mid-deck.* "Adjusted target" after a miss kills the entire KPI system.
> - *Function deep-dives that don't answer the hard question.* Everyone presents, nothing decides.
> - *No kills.* A quarter where the only move is "add" is a quarter without judgment.
> - *Board leaves without three dated commitments.* The meeting didn't happen.

## ✅ Example: "filled-in" version

**FY26-Q1 · Presented 2026-04-22 · Audience: Board + ELT · Presenters: [[CMO]], [[Head-Analytics-Data]]**

**Scorecard:**

| KPI | Target | Actual | Variance | Trend |
|-----|--------|--------|----------|-------|
| Marketing-Sourced ARR | $18.0M | $19.4M | +7.8% | ↑ |
| Pipeline Contribution | 55% | 58% | +3pp | ↑ |
| LTV:CAC | 3.0 | 3.2 | +0.2 | ↑ |
| CAC | $2,400 | $2,210 | -7.9% | ↑ |
| Brand aided awareness | +1.25pp Q | +0.7pp | -0.55pp | ↓ |
| NPS | 50 | 54 | +4 | ↑ |
| Marketing ROI | 5:1 | 5.8:1 | +0.8 | ↑ |
| Budget utilization | 95–102% | 97% | in-band | → |

**The hard question:** *Are we over-investing in performance while brand awareness quietly softens?*

**Worked:** (1) Q4→Q1 LinkedIn reallocation — +32% MQLs in mid-market; (2) "Compound" category report — 31k organic sessions, 840 MQLs; (3) Activation email rewrite — +1.3pp activation on Aurora signups.

**Didn't:** (1) Brand aided awareness below target — under-invested in OOH; (2) Event ROI on two sponsorships below 1.5x — cutting both; (3) SEO traffic to /pricing dropped 14% after a schema change — fix shipped 2026-04-10.

**Budget reconciliation:** reallocate $600k from mid-funnel paid (underperforming on LTV:CAC) into brand OOH + creator program for Q2. See [[Decisions#Quarterly Reallocation]].

**Q2 theme:** *"Concentrate, don't diversify."*
- Bets: (1) OOH in 3 cities tied to brand tracker; (2) Creator program with 12 mid-tier partners; (3) Aurora 2.0 launch at T=2026-09-15.
- Kills: two event sponsorships; the "General SaaS" LinkedIn audience; the Medium newsletter sponsorship experiment.

**Commitments out the door:**

| # | Decision | Owner | Committed | Check-in |
|---|----------|-------|-----------|----------|
| 1 | Shift $600k from paid mid-funnel to brand + creators | [[CMO]] | 2026-04-22 | weekly until 2026-05-31 |
| 2 | Publish brand tracker methodology to board monthly | [[Brand-Manager]] | 2026-04-22 | monthly |
| 3 | Launch Aurora 2.0 GTM doc review | [[VP-Product-Marketing]] | 2026-04-22 | bi-weekly |

Approved: [[CMO]] + CEO 2026-04-22 16:40 PT.
