# 📄 Template: Creative Brief

> [!QUOTE] **The Creed of Craft**
> *A creative brief is a box we build so the work can break out of it. No box — no breakout.*

---

**Owner role:** [[Creative-Director]] · [[Senior-Copywriter]] · [[Art-Director]]
**Used in workflow:** [[Workflows#Brand Approval]]
**Review cadence:** Kickoff → Concept lock (T-14) → V1 review (T-10) → Brand sign-off (T-5)

---

## 🧭 How to use this template

- Open after the [[Campaign-Brief]] is locked. A creative brief without an upstream campaign brief is untethered.
- [[Creative-Director]] writes; [[Senior-Copywriter]] and [[Art-Director]] co-sign before work starts.
- Approval path: [[Creative-Director]] drafts → [[Brand-Manager]] checks guidelines → [[VP-Brand-Strategy]] signs.
- "Done" means: one idea, one tone, one message hierarchy, one deliverables list, zero "let's see what comes back."
- If the brief takes more than 45 minutes to read, it's a spec, not a brief. Cut it.

## ⚠️ Common mistakes

> [!WARNING] **How creative dies on the vine**
> - *Three "single ideas."* There is exactly one. If you cannot choose, you have not understood the audience.
> - *The brief is a wishlist, not a box.* "Make it feel premium but also fun but also urgent but also warm." Pick two.
> - *Proof points stacked like a deck.* The audience remembers one. Choose the one.
> - *Legal invited after concept lock.* Claim rewritten 72 hours before shoot. Creative loses the edge.
> - *Rewriting the brief after seeing rushes.* See [[Manifesto]] Article VII — the brief is sacred.

## ✅ Example: "filled-in" version

- **Project name:** "Compound Your Category" — hero film + ad set for Q3-Compound
- **Linked campaign:** [[Campaign-Brief]] Q3-Compound
- **In-market:** 2026-08-05

**The one idea:** *Brand is the interest rate. Performance is the principal.*
**Audience thinks today:** "Brand is a luxury my CFO won't fund."
**We want her to think:** "Brand is the multiplier on every dollar of performance I already spend."
**Believe us because:** Forrester TEI 412% ROI across 14 SaaS companies.

**Who we're talking to:** Maya, VP Marketing, 400-person SaaS, just lost a board debate on brand spend.
**Where:** LinkedIn feed, 7:30am Tuesday, between two performance-marketing hot takes.
**Share-over-coffee line:** *"Finally someone said what I've been trying to get my CFO to hear."*

**Tone:** Confident. Dry. A little dangerous.
**Voice references:** Stripe press, early Mailchimp, the Economist cover copy.
**Guardrails:** no competitor logos; no fear framing; our colorway only (Ink #0E1116, Signal #E94560).
**Legal:** TEI stat requires footnote + Forrester attribution within 2 scroll-heights.

| Level | Message |
|-------|---------|
| Headline | Brand is the interest rate. |
| Sub | Performance is the principal. Compound both. |
| Proof 1 | 412% ROI across 14 SaaS orgs (Forrester TEI, 2026). |
| Proof 2 | Aurora: 3.4x pipeline in 90 days. |
| CTA | Download the 2026 Compound Report. |

**Deliverables:** 30s film + 6s cutdown; 5 statics (1080²+1200x628); LP; 3-email sequence; 6s/15s social cutdowns. Shoot window 2026-06-22/24. Concept lock 2026-06-15 with [[VP-Brand-Strategy]]. Quality bar: the Stripe-press test.
