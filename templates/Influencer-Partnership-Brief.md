# 📄 Template: Influencer Partnership Brief

> [!QUOTE] **The Creed of the Creator**
> *You are not renting an audience. You are borrowing a relationship. Honor it or lose it.*

---

**Owner role:** [[Influencer-Marketing-Manager]]
**Used in workflow:** [[Workflows#Campaign Launch]]
**Review cadence:** Partner sourced → Brief agreed → Content draft review → Publish → Performance readout → Renewal decision

## 🧭 How to use this template

- Open once a creator has verbally agreed to explore partnership. No brief before agreement — no agreement without a brief.
- [[Influencer-Marketing-Manager]] drafts; [[Brand-Manager]] voice check; Legal reviews disclosure + IP clauses.
- Approval path: IMM → [[Social-Media-Director]] → [[VP-Content-Social]] for spend >$25k → [[CMO]] for spend >$100k or category exclusives.
- "Done" brief: one deliverable set, one success metric, one usage rights scope, one disclosure standard, one kill clause.
- Never approve creative that hides the partnership. FTC disclosure is non-negotiable. See [[Manifesto]] Article III — taste is a responsibility.

## ⚠️ Common mistakes

> [!WARNING] **How creator deals burn trust**
> - *Over-briefing into brand voice.* You hired them for their voice. Let them use it.
> - *Hidden disclosure.* #ad buried in a thread of tags is an FTC case waiting.
> - *No exclusivity or a 12-month blanket one.* Either the creator promotes your competitor next week, or you've priced yourself out of their next post.
> - *Fee without performance bonus.* You're renting the audience; you should share in the result.
> - *"Sponsored content" that looks nothing like the creator's normal output.* Audience spots it in 3 seconds, trust collapses.

## ✅ Example: "filled-in" version

- **Creator:** "Maya Writes Marketing" (@mayawrites) · **Tier:** micro (87k LinkedIn, 42k Substack) · **Platforms:** LinkedIn + Substack · **ER:** 6.8% LinkedIn (3x category) · **Audience match:** 71% to ICP (mid-market B2B mktg leaders) · **Agent:** none, direct

**Why her, why now:** Maya has published four LinkedIn essays in 90 days critical of single-model attribution — her POV aligns with Aurora 2.0's "two-model truth" pillar, and her audience is exactly our beachhead. Linked campaign: [[Launch-GTM-Plan]] Aurora 2.0. Role: **credibility + demo**. Brand safety: 12-month scan clean ([[Brand-Manager]] 2026-07-08).

**Deliverables:**

| Deliverable | Platform | Spec | Rights | Due |
|-------------|----------|------|--------|-----|
| 1x essay | Substack | 1,200–1,800 words, includes 1 demo GIF | perpetual organic, 90d paid | 2026-09-10 |
| 1x post | LinkedIn | native text + 1 image, no crosspost lift | perpetual organic, 90d paid boost | 2026-09-12 |
| 2x comment-thread replies | LinkedIn | 48h post-publish | — | 2026-09-14 |
| raw screen-record footage | handoff | 4K, no edits | brand-owned perpetual | 2026-09-15 |

**Message:** *"Stop arguing about the model. Run two, report the delta."*
**Must-include:** (1) two-model demo beat; (2) phrase "compounding analytics"; (3) CTA to the 2026 Compound Report.
**Must-avoid:** no competitor names, no pricing claims, no "best" superlatives.
**Tone:** dry, confident, a-little-dangerous. FTC: "#ad · partner: [Brand]" in first line of post + Substack top-of-article.

**Commercials:** $18,000 fee. Bonus: +$3,000 at 250k LI impressions, +$5,000 at 500k. Payment: 50/50 net-30. Exclusivity: 90-day category (named: Incumbent A, Incumbent B). Whitelisting: 90 days, $15k spend cap, owned by [[Paid-Media-Manager]]. Kill clause: any content within 90 days attacking the two-model framing, or any undisclosed competing partnership.

**KPIs:** [[KPIs#Social Media]] Influencer ROI target **4x**. Reach: 400k combined impressions. Engagement: ≥6% (her baseline). Conversion: 800 report downloads via UTM `src=mayawrites_mkt_campaign=aurora2`.

**Review:** draft due 2026-09-07; feedback 2026-09-08; publish 2026-09-10 / 2026-09-12. Approver [[Influencer-Marketing-Manager]]; $18k + $15k boost > $25k → signed by [[VP-Content-Social]] 2026-07-15.

Result T+30: 612k impressions, 8.1% ER, 1,140 report downloads, 186 MQLs, 41 opps, $820k pipeline — **4.6x ROI**. Renewal recommended.
