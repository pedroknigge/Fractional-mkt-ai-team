# 📄 Template: Content Brief

> [!QUOTE] **The Creed of the Written Word**
> *Content without a brief is a monologue. A brief makes it a conversation with intent.*

---

**Owner role:** [[Content-Strategy-Director]] · [[Content-Writer]] · [[SEO-SEM-Specialist]]
**Used in workflow:** [[Workflows#Content Pipeline]]
**Review cadence:** Brief approval → Outline check → Draft review → Publish

---

## 🧭 How to use this template

- Open once an editorial slot is confirmed on the [[Content-Strategy-Director]]'s calendar. No slot, no brief.
- [[SEO-SEM-Specialist]] supplies search data; [[Content-Strategy-Director]] supplies angle; writer owns execution.
- Approval path: [[Content-Strategy-Director]] → [[SEO-SEM-Specialist]] keyword sign-off → [[Brand-Manager]] voice check (for pillar pieces only) → publish.
- "Done" brief: one search intent, one reader, one CTA, one owner-for-distribution.
- If nobody on the distribution side has signed the "Distribution plan" row, the piece does not get published. See [[Manifesto]] Article VIII.

## ⚠️ Common mistakes

> [!WARNING] **How content briefs go sideways**
> - *Keyword chosen without intent analysis.* Piece ranks for a query the reader isn't actually asking.
> - *Outline negotiated mid-draft.* Rewrite costs 2x the original draft and loses the thesis.
> - *"We'll figure out distribution after it publishes."* You won't. 80% of ROI dies in silence.
> - *Two CTAs of equal weight.* Reader picks zero.
> - *SME interview booked after draft 1.* The draft has to be rewritten to match what the SME actually said.

## ✅ Example: "filled-in" version

- **Working title:** "Why Your Attribution Model Is Lying to You"
- **Content type:** Pillar (MOFU) · Slot: week of 2026-05-04 · Target 2,100 words

**Search:** Primary kw *"marketing attribution models"* (4,400/mo, KD 48). Secondary: *"multi-touch attribution"* (1,900), *"marketing mix modeling vs attribution"* (880), *"attribution model comparison"* (540). Intent: Informational→Commercial. SERP top 3: HubSpot, Bizible, Ruler — all explainers, none take a stance. Target: Top 3 by month 4.

**Reader:** Maya, VP Mktg, 400-FTE SaaS. Knows first-touch/last-touch/multi-touch. Googling because her CFO asked "why can't you tell me which channel works?" Wants an opinion she can defend in a meeting. Success action: downloads the "Attribution Decision Tree" (gated).

**Thesis:** Most attribution models optimize for the CMO's comfort, not the company's reality. The right model is the one whose errors you can explain.
**Why now:** iOS 17 + cookie deprecation have broken last-touch; Q2 2026 is the moment to pick a new default.
**Contrarian hook:** Abandon single-model fidelity; run two models in parallel and report the delta.
**Proof:** Aurora case (switched models, reallocated $600k, +18% pipeline); Bessemer benchmark report; MIT Sloan 2025 paper.
**Avoid:** "MMM is back" takes, anything citing Google's own attribution doc.

**Outline:** 1) Hook — the CFO question; 2) Why every model lies; 3) The four real choices; 4) Aurora's two-model playbook; 5) The objection ("this is too much work"); 6) The decision tree + CTA.

**KPIs:** [[KPIs#Content]] — 8k organic sessions/mo by M6; content conversion rate >3%. Business: 120 MQLs in 90 days.
**CTA:** Download the Attribution Decision Tree (gated PDF). UTM `src=organic_mkt_campaign=attribution-pillar`.

**Distribution:** Newsletter 2026-05-06 feature · LinkedIn carousel 2026-05-07 by [[Social-Media-Manager]] · sales one-pager by [[Product-Marketing-Manager]] 2026-05-09 · PR pitch to MarTech Today + Demand Gen Report by [[PR-Communications-Director]] · retargeting set by [[Paid-Media-Manager]] $15k over 30 days.

SME: Head-Analytics-Data interview booked 2026-04-22. Approver: [[Content-Strategy-Director]].
